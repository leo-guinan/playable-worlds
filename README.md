---

*This book traces a lineage of thinkers who transformed mathematics from isolated insight into a system that could scale across centuries, institutions, and eventually machines.*

*Four of those thinkers — Lovelace, Noether, Hopper, Goldwasser — were excluded from the arenas they helped build. Their responses to that exclusion are as instructive as their mathematics.*
